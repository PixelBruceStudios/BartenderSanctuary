- Why influential: author of *The PDT Cocktail Book*, former PDT beverage director, helped define the modern speakeasy format.
- Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/jimmeehan/
- Sources: https://pdtcocktails.com/, https://www.diffordsguide.com/contributors/jim-meehan, https://www.liquor.com/authors/jim-meehan-4800009/
